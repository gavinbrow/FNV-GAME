# Prospective UUID parent EH claim accounting

This source-only helper checks authored metadata claims for the fixed seven-root scope. It reads no files, starts no process and imports no observer or parser. It does not replay native evidence or produce a closure certificate.

`qualify_prospective_parent_eh(bundle, root_address, anchors=...)` requires the complete seven-root roster and fourteen distinct metadata sites. Separate caller-supplied anchors bind the input digests; those expectations are explicitly unverified by this helper. A caller inventing both claims and anchors has proved nothing.

Each root declares its parent and owned code/state sections, two code cuts of 11 and 27 bytes, full physical field inventory and target records, two metadata sites, inherited blocker occurrences and typed evidence. The field inventory is asserted completeness, compared against the supplied records; it is not independently enumerated from COFF here. Physical indices, offsets, relocation kinds, addends and target identity remain distinct. Shared original bases cannot silently merge canonical domains or semantic subtypes.

The six obligation classes are the complete field/target census; metadata HIGHLOW ownership; handler/cookie/cleanup semantics; parent-frame/no-catch family; source/compiler/object lineage; and expanded omission/namespace/safety closure. HIGHLOW produces two separate site occurrences per parent. Inherited blocker order and repeated IDs retain their occurrence ordinals.

A missing, unknown or false evidence row retains that status and its distinct reason. Malformed joins, stale anchors, foreign parents, hidden fields and role conflicts reject the input. An ordinary MATCH or an empty diagnostic list cannot hide a false helper piece. Current source hashes alone do not establish historical compiler-to-object source lineage. A prior eight-byte cleanup family cannot establish the observed eleven-byte family.

Even fully consistent authored inputs return only `schema_complete` and `authored_claims_consistent`. Every obligation remains open with `SOURCE_PHASE_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_REPLAYED`; `discharged` is always empty. Runtime, provider, ABI, frame execution, projection and full-closure flags remain false. A well-formed negative or missing-evidence input can have valid schema while its claims are inconsistent; `evidence_inventory_complete` distinguishes absent rows.

The prior seven-root observation is immutable. Its reported two integrity passes differ from the three passes on the successful source path; the separate counter-correction plan preserves that deviation. This helper neither changes that report nor authorizes another observation.

Before any future production consumer exists, independently reviewed typed witnesses must supply the missing physical records, PE relocation-directory ownership, handler/cookie/frame semantics, source lineage and expanded graph. The ordinary observer and existing EH tools remain unchanged. The initial authored tests use dictionaries only; no production projection or admission is implemented.

The initial 13 authored cases were insufficient: independent resealed inputs exposed 13 consistency gaps while the no-admission boundary held. That source/test snapshot and its failure receipt remain preserved privately. The successor adds captured FuncInfo/state records, native-to-original cut intervals, effective-addend mapping, complete handler/checker/cookie source-field joins, native domain/type/value checks, strict integers and bounded original addresses. Shared original intervals require a separate ICF policy and are rejected here.

Whole SafeSEH input includes the ordered physical target records, section dimensions, original count and section-aux form. One registration must join the owned handler. Every foreign registration retains either an anchored omission claim or an unresolved occurrence; an unresolved item produces its own open graph obligation. Whole-table native inventories and physical symbol indices must agree between root views of the same object. A boolean that a table was accounted for cannot substitute for these records.

Canonical contract names follow the existing shared code-pool vocabulary: `ordinary-function`, `cxx-frame-handler`, `security-cookie-check`, and `security-cookie`. The concrete native CxxFrameHandler3 symbol remains separate from its contract kind. These are claim-consistency rules, never adoption of a provider or omission policy.
