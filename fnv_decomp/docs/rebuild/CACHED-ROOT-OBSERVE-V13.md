# V13 qualified-context observer preparation

This new module is a source-first opt-in for qualification policy
`cached-CODE-frontier-inventory-v13`. The earlier `a393` observer, its V11/V12
contracts and all accepted outcomes remain unchanged. A V13 outer row is never
renamed to the earlier qualification policy to pass a legacy gate.

The independently accepted metadata qualification contains 72 new roots in 44 recipes,
producing 116 singleton and union views and 31,877 declared code bytes. These
counts describe the whole request, not positive closure results. The new driver
binds the accepted qualification receipt and both outcome reviews. Operational
source review, exact input review and a paired resource lease remain required
before its first original-object observation. No such observation has run.

The version-local root validator and streaming core retain the earlier reviewed
semantics where its fixed qualification policy and 180-root limit prevent direct
reuse. Independent schema, canonicalization and physical-field helpers are reused
from the earlier module. The pure seam performs no file reads and does not enforce
`PREVIOUS_SOURCE_SHA256` itself. The operational dependency gate hashes the earlier
module and its eight fixed helpers before using operational metadata helpers,
and checks their imported paths. Source review binds the current helper bytes
separately. No earlier module globals are mutated. The new
observation policy is `cached-context-root-observation-v13`.

Each qualified outer row, contract, source-frame identity, request provenance and
hash must remain unchanged. The fresh frozen ordinary certificate belongs outside
the qualified evidence. Exact source/object/selected-span identities, physical
relocations and addends, full-unit incoming proofs, singleton partitioning and the
final union remain mandatory. Unsupported COFF formats remain explicit negatives;
contradictory evidence is fatal. No EH, SafeSEH, TLS, directive, label, data, missing
field or namespace waiver is introduced. Runtime, provider and emission flags
remain false even when ordinary closure succeeds.

The driver consumes the complete accepted new request, groups its
recipes, and performs one fresh source hash and raw-object observation/parse per
distinct requested recipe. Historical requested roots and certificates are not
replayed. The 708-root registry and accepted prior summary identify the earlier
77 recipe units without opening the old detail stream. Their intersection with
the 44 requested recipes is reported explicitly: a genuinely new root may share
a previously observed physical unit. Such overlap neither removes the new root
nor substitutes a historical certificate. Whole-unit closure may still describe
neighboring sections. The driver reads no global binding/owner stream or cache
frames and preserves the existing
900-second, 1-GiB measured-memory and 128-MiB combined-output limits, including the
journal. The earlier same-byte gzip authentication, owned-output cleanup,
post-publication stability and journal-close gates are retained. Nineteen fixed
input references include the qualification's canonical-serialized historical
collision epoch and the prior native summary, alongside the new qualification
and frontier. A separate current UUID epoch and its accepted independent peer
are bound without restamping the historical epoch. The join preserves all 14,534
historical records and their order, permits only the reviewed typed UUID append,
and checks current counts of 14,529 ordinary and 14,535 broader exclusions.
The 72 requested names must remain disjoint from that current namespace. The
UUID row keeps its original negative and qualified provenance; it is not treated
as a bare ordinary positive. No original UUID input or producer is replayed.
Prepared counts establish no application or runtime adoption.

The accepted qualification excludes recipe 2768. Its separately owned UUID
operation is not replayed here. Any changed final request containing that recipe
requires an explicit coordinated shared-unit read plan before the native-read
gate; neither a prior projection nor this document silently grants that scope.

Authored tests exercise honest V13 policy identity, unchanged envelopes, direct
frozen-observer equivalence, one parse per recipe, legacy-policy rejection,
strict physical/source identity, field addends, full-unit import context,
certificate-bearing metadata rejection, partial batches, missing-profile and
missing-acceptance gates, dependency drift and operation policy/profile borrowing.
They read no saved qualification inputs, original objects, images or game
source bodies.
