# Batch 8: array declaration review

The three unsized array declarations have independent source access counts and structure stride evidence. Those counts prove the span accessed by the observed loops; they do not prove where the complete original object ends. No array is selected as a complete definition by this review. Details and machine-readable results are in `inventory/b8_arrays_review.json`.

- **`DAT_01190910`**: `_CommandEntry` in `fnv_decomp/src/fallout_shared/tesparameters.cpp:183` has a 20-byte prefix, a 32-bit pointer at `+0x14`, and a 16-byte suffix, giving a 40-byte x86 stride. `CommandDescription` in `tesscriptfunctions.cpp:29120` is also 40 bytes; its named fields describe the first 16 bytes and the remaining six words are opaque. That file loops over 640 entries at line 29158, supporting an accessed range `[0x01190910, 0x01196d10)` of 25,600 bytes. This span has 3,802 original relocation sites, all at offsets 0, 4, 12, 20, 24, 28 or 32 within records. There are no overlaps with the frozen Batch 7, DV1, or produced ranges. The source views do not establish complete field types or exact array extent.
- **`DAT_0118e8e0`**: the local compiler declaration uses a 40-byte opaque `ScriptFunctionInfo`, while `fnv_decomp/include/ScriptFunctionInfo.h` documents a 40-byte layout and static assertions for its offsets. The `CommandDescription` view also has a 40-byte stride. Loops in `tesscriptfunctions.cpp:29148` and `tesscriptcompiler.cpp:692`, and the opcode bounds at `tesscriptcompiler.cpp:21`, independently support 205 entries, or `[0x0118e8e0, 0x011908e8)` (8,200 bytes). Its 1,122 relocations lie at per-record offsets 0, 4, 12, 20, 24 and 28. Existing DV1/produced ownership covers `[0x011906e8, 0x011906ec)` and Batch 7 owns `[0x011906ec, 0x011908f0)`, overlapping the accessed span. Whole-span selection therefore requires an ownership change and revalidation. These views and loops do not establish the full array extent.
- **`DAT_01187ac8`**: `bgsprimitive.cpp:2775` declares a structure of three doubles, giving a 24-byte stride; `SetRadius` reads all three fields for 112 elements at lines 2796–2800. The accessed range is `[0x01187ac8, 0x01188548)` (2,688 bytes), with no Batch 7, frozen DV1, or produced ownership overlap and no original relocations. Its 336 reference doubles are finite; eight are negative zero, so literal generation must preserve their sign bits and be checked against compiled bytes. The unsized declaration and loop do not establish the full array bound.

The unowned accessed spans could be investigated as typed **prefixes** under synthetic names, without changing decompiled source, but a prefix must not be represented as a proven complete original array. Before selection, check field types and relocation targets, all boundary and directory constraints, compiler output including negative-zero encoding, and the image round trip. The overlapping second span also needs an explicit ownership plan. No compilation, selection, build, or gameplay test was performed for these arrays.
