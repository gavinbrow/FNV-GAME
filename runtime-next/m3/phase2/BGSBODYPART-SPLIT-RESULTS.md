Both units' original and candidate `.xdata$x` section contents and relocations agree. Their `.text$x` cleanup thunk bytes agree, but direct destructor symbol spellings differ; candidate targets must be resolved in final linkage and checked against the reference EH graph. Matching mapped functions and graph prefixes alone do not prove a complete native class or runtime image.

The separate `FUN_005e3c60_cls` destructor adapter remains in this compiled candidate. It intentionally does not merge unrelated deleting/free wrappers. Its generated body, member cleanups and EH metadata must be compared before the adapter can be removed from an integration proposal.
