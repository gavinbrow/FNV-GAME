A technically small first experiment is unit 1108 (`src/fallout_shared/tesresponse.cpp`, 11 mapped functions). Its `tesresponse_component.h` has the exact class body, explicit pack 4 and size assertion; its only recorded recipe consumer is unit 1108. Its five-file quoted closure and hashes are in `../out/phase2-baseformcomponent-pilot.json`. `tesresponse_decls.h` and `tesresponse_forms.h` already include `tesresponse_support.h` directly, and the local component header also includes it. That support header owns the `FNV_LAYOUT_SUPPORT_H` guard and supplies much more than the assertion macro, including templates and forward declarations. A replacement must retain this local support include and its ordering; blindly pulling in the canonical `layout_support.h` or relying on its contents can change definitions behind the shared guard.

A possible guarded design is a new self-contained internal class-definition header with only the exact four virtual declarations, explicit pack 4 and an independent uniquely named four-byte typedef assertion. Keep the public `include/BaseFormComponent.h` as a compatibility wrapper that still includes its existing `layout_support.h` before the class-definition header; keep `tesresponse_component.h` as a local wrapper that still includes `tesresponse_support.h` before that same class-definition header. Preserve the existing `FNV_BASEFORMCOMPONENT_H` wrapper guard and give the inner header a distinct guard; forbid direct inner-header use until guard interactions are proven. This avoids silently dropping transitive declarations. It is a proposal, not a prepared source change: users that relied on the public wrapper's transitive support still receive it. The 159 lexical recipe users of the canonical wrapper plus the one separate unit 1108 are an over-approximate 160-unit affected cohort if the wrapper changes, not an 11-function-only integration. Their full source/closure, flag and production object checks, including local literal-label rederivation, are required before installation.
