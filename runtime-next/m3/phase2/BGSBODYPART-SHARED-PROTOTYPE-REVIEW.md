The grouped shared-model and shared-body fixtures each compiled all four original/mirror objects. Their saved unit 620 comparator reports 13 matches and two unresolved candidate relocations; with the independently grounded experimental PSA-vtable and virtual TESModel-destructor bindings, direct reference comparison gives 15/15. Unit 621 has 10/10 original and candidate matches. The grouped body fixture also changes the named `QueueResourceLoad` symbol to virtual at the observed first BGSBodyPart slot.

The existing structural comparator reports both the candidate BGSBodyPart graph and its one observed slot match, and the PSA graph and eight observed slots match under the explicit experimental source-backed bindings saved in `graph-unit.json`. These are metadata comparison results, not proof of canonical source identity, original complete table extent, final placement, or linker weak resolution. The emitted implicit PSA ordinary and scalar deleting destructor sections have 19 and 44 bytes respectively; their earlier independent reference comparison must be repeated on the actual grouped objects before accepting them.

Both unit 620 `.xdata$x` section multisets match; its cleanup thunk bytes match but two relocation symbol spellings change. Unit 621 `.xdata$x` matches; one cleanup thunk has matching bytes and changes a direct TESModel destructor relocation from nonvirtual to virtual spelling. The original `0x00489070` target is supported but all exact relocated EH targets and weak external resolution need explicit validation at integration.

The five-header split proposal places FileEntryList, TESModel, TESModelPSA, BodyPartInfo, and BGSBodyPart in separate private headers, with the same content mirrored into both units. It was staged and frozen after the grouped fixture; it is not yet compiled. A production payload must revalidate its include closures, all 15+10 functions, constructor and destructor code, RTTI, vtable, EH and weak aliases, and then meet full link and runtime gates. The grouped fixture is not counted as completed native header recovery.
