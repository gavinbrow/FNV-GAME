# BFC ordinary EH blockers

The archived d569 production objects and both mirror sets produce the same ordinary-oracle result: 364/367 listed tail extents pass, 73 of those using inferred targets, and zero inferred-name conflicts across the cohort. The three failed extents are not candidate-specific; every section that the oracle locates for their own parent matches, but each listed extent continues into non-padding code that those anchors do not locate.

* Unit 958, `TESAmmo` destructor surrogate at 0x00502880: the matched region ends at 0x00ef3940; the 43-byte remainder contains two cleanup jumps and an EH handler. The archived object's located parent section ends before those cleanups. Their source/ownership is not established.
* Unit 1027, `TESLevCharacter::Load` at 0x0050a930: the matched region ends at 0x00ef3c70; the following 57 bytes comprise three cleanup jumps and a handler. The full remainder matches, with relocation fields masked, the same-unit archived `.text$x` section emitted for the separate `FUN_0050a770` constructor. Its regular parent anchor places that constructor's emitted section elsewhere; a second reference placement and its xdata/ownership need independent proof. Byte similarity alone does not establish placement or ownership.
* Unit 1032, `TESLevItem::Load` at 0x0050b560: the matched region ends at 0x00ef3d10; the following 49 bytes contain two additional delete-call cleanup funclets and a handler. The checked source uses a newly allocated `ContainerItemExtra`; the emitted parent section covers only the earlier anchored fragments. Matching short handler fragments is insufficient to identify the unanchored remainder as a complete source-supported contribution.

The oracle also reports 12 target errors in located sections of units 253, 258, 260, 273 and 370, including sections outside the three extents. Several are clear limitations in treating identical decorated local surrogate names as global identity: for example `??1RefPtr@@QAE@XZ` has a global recipe binding of 0x0044cbf0 while unit 260's original tail calls 0x0045cec0 and 0x00b66f20 at separate sites. Unit 258 has unresolved `ArrayStorage` and `ScratchArray` destructors. These source-local identities and extents require distinct owner and call-target evidence; reference target values alone cannot justify global or unit bindings. One unit 253 observed displacement resolves outside plausible image code and needs independent placement review rather than a guessed deletion target.

The audited source, objects and recipe remain unchanged. Completion requires either independently anchored same-unit contribution and ownership proof for the unlocated fragments or faithful source reconstruction that emits them, plus source-supported per-call identities for the other target errors. Do not narrow listed extents, conflate names across translation units, or count masked byte similarity as a complete EH match.
